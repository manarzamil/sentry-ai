# 3D Product Model

Concept renders of the Sentry AI enclosure mounted on an industrial hard hat. These images show the intended physical integration of the electronics; they are design renders, not photographs of the assembled prototype.

| File | View | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| `sentry-ai-helmet-render-front.png` | Front elevation | Enclosure and GPS module on the shell crown, cable routing to the internal board, battery cell and controller board under the shell |
| `sentry-ai-helmet-render-angle.png` | Three-quarter top | Enclosure footprint relative to the crown and the retention strap, plus the aerial position of the GPS module |

Source: interactive model at <https://studio.tripo3d.ai/3d-model/c44f07f1-ff50-4713-a6bd-9200887e1228?invite_code=KHUC1Y>

Mounting note: any real hard-hat modification must not breach the shell or compromise its certified impact rating. The renders locate the enclosure on the crown for GPS sky view, which is a design intent and not a validated mounting method.
